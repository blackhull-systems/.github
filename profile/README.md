**Blackhull measures the agent economy, and sells AI agents the checks they run before they pay or trade.**

AI agents now pay for services on their own, one call at a time. Blackhull reads those payments from the public chain
and sells what it finds, per call over [x402](https://x402.org), with the evidence behind every answer. Facts, not
financial advice.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Token check** | Who controls a Base token, whether it can mint, pause, blacklist or change fees, whether its code can be replaced, and its liquidity: read live from the chain. $0.01 |
| **Service check** | Whether a paid x402 service is really paid at its listed price, by how many independent buyers, and whether its demand is concentrated or self-funded. From $0.003 |
| **Routing** | The best-rated providers for a job within a budget, ranked neutrally. $0.003 |

finding for free and every outcome is public; when we get something wrong, the correction is dated and published.
